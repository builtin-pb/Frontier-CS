## Literature route and final-byte calibration

This is a clean-room adaptation of the error-free-subset, linear-solve, and
retry route in [Sun--Tibouchi--Abe, ePrint 2020/666,
Section 4.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/666). Under this record's exact
15-of-222 support law, the clean event is `C(m-w,n)/C(m,n)`, namely
`C(207,74)/C(222,74)`. Neither the paper's uniform-matrix rank calculation nor
its asymptotic runtime transfers to this finite release; minor rank and the
catalog rate remain independently uncertain.

For these final bytes, the first `bounded_error` probe was censored at the
16-second E2 lower edge. The subsequent full replay in
[`CALIBRATION.md`](../CALIBRATION.md) censored seed 0 at 90 seconds, recovered
the witness at seed 1 under the same cap, and passed the production evaluator.
The randomized concurrent replay retained no portable per-instance wall time,
so the public fields remain `calibration_status="extrapolated"` and
`measured_runtime_seconds=null`.

## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

The clean equations determine `s` over `F_4093` without enumerating binary
assignments. Consequently this attack uses only sparse-error support and treats
the iid binary secret as an after-the-fact validity check. The ignored
structure leaves binary enumeration, hybrid guessing, and joint sparse-error/
small-secret methods as live alternatives; the large nonzero-error alphabet
can change their filtering and memory costs.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` for at most two seconds
and recovered no witness. Because the run was capped, it shows only that this
configuration found no immediate solution; it does not estimate primal cost
or rank primal against clean-subset. The prediction is not established as the
fastest attack or a lower bound. The matrix is genuinely uniform, hence sparse-matrix lower-bound
results are irrelevant. Binary-/entropy-LWE comparisons also do not transfer
without a Gaussian target-error map for this exact-support bounded error.
Status is `extrapolated`, measured runtime is null, and no hardness claim is
made.
