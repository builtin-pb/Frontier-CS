## Interaction and alternative attacks

Enumeration is a complete upper bound because it covers every secret admitted
by the verifier, independently of the matrix distribution.  Dense `A` still
affects implementation constants: streaming rejection, column-sum caching,
and the broad 65-value residual window can change actual throughput.  The
model's `1/n` rate scaling does not separately model `m`, rejection depth, or
those interactions.

A 31+32 support split has 4,992 and 5,489 partial subsets of weights at most
three.  Nonzero error invalidates a direct exact-key MITM join, while even four
approximate matching coordinates admit `65^4=17,850,625` error offsets.  MITM
is unpriced and could win with a better multi-stage filter, but a naive join has
a large collision/error neighborhood and no established crossover.  The exact planted zero-error
mass is about 0.0374872 per coordinate, so an all-clean 63-row subsystem has
probability about `2^-298.460`; clean-subset solving is not competitive under
that model.  Primal BDD or hybrid lattice attacks remain possible experiments,
not calibrated alternatives in this file.  Full mod-`q` enumeration would be
`257^63 = 2^504.354` trials; that loose fallback is neither a Lattice
Estimator result nor evidence of hardness.
