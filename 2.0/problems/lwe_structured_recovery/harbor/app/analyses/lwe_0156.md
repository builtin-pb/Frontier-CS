## Interaction and limits of the claim

The estimator's uniform-q-ary matrix is only a heuristic normal-LWE proxy for sparse `A`. It gives neither a reduction-backed upper bound nor evidence of hardness for this record. Signed cancellation can make partial-sum methods more effective, while sparse rows make every update cheaper. The observed matrix has no repeated support or row; this removes only the most direct cancellation construction, not graph, syndrome, or mixed lattice attacks. The raw support universe is `C(110,21)=2^73.996`.

The matrix also fails the `o(sqrt(n))` condition of the [sparse-LWE lower-bound result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512), and the planted balanced exact-weight signed secret differs from its uniform q-ary target. BBTV is not instantiated; matrix and secret structure occur together, their effects are not assumed independent, and no hardness claim is made. Planted entropy is 69.095 bits, compared with 71.350 bits for the verifier language; both are concrete counts and no [entropy-LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119) hardness transfer is used. The H6 value is an extrapolation controlled by the uniform-to-sparse proxy and the rop-to-time convention.
