## Interaction and limits of the claim

Uniform `A` in this estimator run is a heuristic normal-LWE proxy for the sparse q-ary release. It is not a reduction-backed upper bound and makes no hardness claim. Sparse multiplication, support-graph attacks, and binary guessing can interact; no proof says their costs multiply. On this seed, no row support repeats and `C(130,23)=2^84.076` possible supports makes whole-support exhaustion unattractive, but partial structures remain unpriced.

The row weight is `k=23=ceil(2*sqrt(130))`, with `k^2/n=529/130=4.0692`; hence BBTV's finite retained-sample factor `1-3/l-k^2/n` is negative for every positive `l`. The matrix is also not in the `k=o(sqrt(n))` premise of the [sparse-LWE lower-bound result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512), and the planted exact-weight binary secret is not its uniform q-ary target. BBTV is not instantiated; secret structure is changed simultaneously, and this scope check is not a hardness claim. Exact secret entropy is 60.923 bits, already captured by direct enumeration rather than an [entropy-LWE lower bound](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119). The principal uncertainties are the estimator's uniform-matrix substitution, practical hybrid constants, and residual pruning in this particular degree profile.
