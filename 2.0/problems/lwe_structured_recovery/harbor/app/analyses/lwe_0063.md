`sparse_uniform` matrix rows contain exactly seven nonzero residues. The secret

## Literature route and final-byte calibration

This is a clean-room adaptation of the error-free-subset, linear-solve, and
retry route in [Sun--Tibouchi--Abe, ePrint 2020/666,
Section 4.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/666). The exact event for the fixed
15-of-30 support is `C(m-w,n)/C(m,n) = C(15,10)/C(30,10)`. The paper's
uniform-matrix rank calculation and asymptotic runtime are not transferred to
this sparse finite matrix; repeated supports, clean-minor rank, and the high
small-`n` throughput extrapolation remain local uncertainties.

The first final-byte `bounded_error` probe was censored at the 16-second E2
lower edge. The subsequent full replay in
[`CALIBRATION.md`](../CALIBRATION.md) censored seeds 0--2 at 90 seconds and
recovered the witness at seed 3 under the same cap; the production evaluator
accepted it. Because this randomized concurrent replay retained no portable

## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

The secret is unrestricted q-ary, so the clean field solve has no secret
promise to exploit. It uses error sparsity alone and leaves row-support/error-
support interaction, cancellation among the repeated supports, and decoding
the 20-dimensional left-kernel syndrome as alternate research routes. At this
dimension even exhaustive or meet-in-the-middle treatments of error support
and magnitudes deserve empirical comparison.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` with a two-second cap and
recovered no witness. At this especially small dimension, that censored
non-recovery remains only smoke evidence; it does not estimate primal cost or
rank it against clean-subset. The catalog number is not a lower bound. Row weight
satisfies `7/sqrt(10)=2.21359`, not `o(sqrt(n))`, so the cited sparse-LWE
lower-bound premise is absent. Small dimension and nonstandard exact-support
error further preclude any hardness inference. Calibration is `extrapolated`
and measured runtime is null.
