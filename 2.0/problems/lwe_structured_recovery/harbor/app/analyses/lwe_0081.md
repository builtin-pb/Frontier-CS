clean with exact probability

`p = C(107,39) / C(117,39) = 0.0141072971132631`.

This route is explicitly an adaptation of
[Sun--Tibouchi--Abe, ePrint 2020/666, Section 4.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/666):

and retry. With exactly 10 planted dirty rows, the event here is the exact
hypergeometric `C(m-w,n)/C(m,n)` displayed above. The paper's uniform-matrix
rank behavior and asymptotic runtime do not transfer to this sparse signed
public matrix; selected-minor rank and solve throughput remain
instance/repository assumptions.
