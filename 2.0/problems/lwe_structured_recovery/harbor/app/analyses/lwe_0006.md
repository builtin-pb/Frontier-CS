## Interaction and alternative attacks

The direct work factor is a complete, matrix-independent upper bound because
it exhausts every secret admitted by the verifier.  Candidate throughput is
instance-dependent: dense column additions, a 92-row validation, rejection
ordering, and the 65-value (roughly quarter-modulus) residual interval all
matter.  The spec's `1/n` scaling does not explicitly price `m` or those
interactions.

A 21+21 split has 401,930 partial supports per side through weight eight.
Nonzero error rules out an ordinary exact-key MITM join; even four approximate
coordinates permit `65^4=17,850,625` offsets.  MITM is unpriced and may beat
enumeration with a careful multi-stage filter, but no such crossover is
measured here, and naive joins pay material memory and collision costs.  Since
bounded-uniform error is zero with probability `1/65`, an all-clean 42-row
subsystem has probability `65^-42 = 2^-252.939`, so clean-subset solving is not
the selected route.  Primal BDD and lattice hybrids remain separate unmeasured
experiments.  The universal mod-`q` fallback is
`257^42 = 2^336.236` candidates; it is only a loose upper bound, never a
hardness floor or a Lattice Estimator result.
