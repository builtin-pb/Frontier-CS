## Interaction, alternatives, and bounded screen

The binary matrix makes row products addition-only but does not change the
secret to an `F_2` vector; all recovery arithmetic is over `F_257`. With
roughly half-dense rows the sparse-matrix lower-bound result does not apply,
and the full modular secret is not a low-entropy plant. The binary matrix is
also nonuniform relative to standard q-ary `A`, so no ordinary-LWE reduction
