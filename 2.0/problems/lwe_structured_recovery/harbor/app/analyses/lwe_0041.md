## Bound public record

This paper-cohort instance has `(n,m,q)=(97,291,1229)`. `A` is dense uniform
over `Z_1229`, expanded with the public SHAKE domain. The planted secret is
`iid_alphabet` on `{0,1}`; the public predicate admits every binary vector
(zero through 97 nonzeros). The `sparse_bounded` error has exactly seven
nonzero coordinates, each in `[-153,-1] union [1,153]`. Verification permits
`max_abs=153`, `L1<=1,071`, `L2^2<=163,863`, and support at most seven.

The released expansion has rank `97/97` modulo 1229, and its first 97 rows
also have rank 97. Across all 28,227 coefficients there are 22 zeros, row
support sizes are 95--97, and all 291 rows are distinct. These seed-specific
rank facts make recovery possible but are not a distributional guarantee for
a randomly selected clean minor.

## Selected clean-subset median model

Choose 97 rows, solve `A_I s=b_I (mod q)`, and validate the candidate on all
291 equations. The recorded formula is
`ln(2)/(p*max(130*(16/n)^1.5,5))`. Conditional on the planted seven-position
error support, a uniform subset is clean with the exact probability

`p = C(291-7,97)/C(291,97) = C(284,97)/C(291,97) = 0.056414764819664405`.

The `analytical-clean-subset-v2` convention uses the continuous median proxy
`ln(2)/p = 12.286627140530701` attempts. The DS_SMALL family rate is
`max(130*(16/97)^1.5,5) = 8.708948550424445` systems/second, hence
`12.286627140530701 / 8.708948550424445 = 1.4108048829765898` seconds.
That prediction lies in `E0=[1,4)` and is close to the 1.414214-second slot
target.

The probability is exact for a fixed seven-row dirty support. The median and
runtime are not: `ln(2)/p` approximates independent with-replacement geometric
trials, and the rate absorbs elimination, validation, and retry costs. It does
not separately charge singular clean minors. Full rank of the whole matrix or
of the first 97 rows does not prove that every sampled clean minor is
invertible.

## Literature route and final-byte calibration

This is a clean-room adaptation of the error-free-subset, linear-solve, and
retry route in [Sun--Tibouchi--Abe, ePrint 2020/666,
Section 4.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/666). For this fixed-weight release,
the exact event is `C(m-w,n)/C(m,n)`, evaluated above as
`C(284,97)/C(291,97)`; it replaces the paper's error model. No uniform-matrix
rank probability or asymptotic runtime from the paper is transferred to this
finite record. Sampled-minor rank and the family throughput remain separate
uncertainties.

The final-byte campaign in [`CALIBRATION.md`](../CALIBRATION.md) records a
successful `bounded_error` recovery with seed 0 in 1.669663 internal seconds;
the recovered vector passed the public verifier. That is useful release
evidence, but the campaign is not a complete E0--E5 benchmark.
The authoritative public fields therefore remain
`calibration_status="extrapolated"` and `measured_runtime_seconds=null`.

## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

A nonsingular clean subsystem recovers `s` as a full field vector, so the
attack never enumerates the `2^97` binary domain. The binary promise is used
only when the recovered candidate is checked. Thus the selected model exploits
error sparsity while deliberately ignoring secret structure; binary
enumeration, binary-secret hybrids, and attacks combining both structures can
change the practical winner. Error magnitudes do not enter `p`, although they
matter to residual validation and syndrome-decoding alternatives.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` with a two-second cap and
recovered no witness. That censored result is only evidence against immediate
recovery under that configuration; it neither estimates general primal cost
nor ranks it against clean-subset. The clean-subset number is not a proven
portfolio minimum or a lower bound. Uniform `A` is outside the sparse-matrix
lower-bound question. Although the iid binary secret is reduction-adjacent,
no Gaussian target-error mapping or complete entropy-/binary-secret reduction
is instantiated for this exact-support bounded error. Calibration status is
`extrapolated`, `measured_runtime_seconds` is null, and no hardness claim is
made.
