## Interaction and limits of the claim

The estimator assumes uniform q-ary `A`. Applying it to the sparse released matrix is a heuristic normal-LWE proxy, not a reduction-backed upper bound and not a hardness result. At this lower dimension, sparse row operations, support-graph elimination, and balanced-sign collision searches deserve particular attention. The realization offers no repeated support for trivial row differencing, while the possible support set `C(75,18)=2^56.408` is still too large for plain support exhaustion.

Here `k=18=ceil(2*sqrt(75))`, so `k^2/n=324/75=4.3200` and BBTV's finite retained-sample factor `1-3/l-k^2/n` is negative for every positive `l`. The matrix also misses the `k=o(sqrt(n))` premise of the [sparse-LWE lower-bound result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512), while the planted balanced exact-weight signed secret differs from its uniform q-ary target. BBTV is not instantiated, and no independence or hardness claim is made. The generator entropy is 60.739 bits and the broader verifier domain is 62.994 bits, both exposed rather than hidden behind the [entropy-LWE lower bound](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119). Because uSVP and hybrid differ by only 0.184 bits in estimated rop, estimator-model noise and sparse-matrix effects can readily swap the practical winner; H0 is consequently an extrapolated placement, not a promise.
