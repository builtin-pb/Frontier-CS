## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

A clean invertible subsystem determines the whole secret modulo 1229 and only
then checks whether it is binary. Clean-subset recovery thus ignores the
`2^66` secret structure. Direct enumeration, binary-secret hybrids, and joint
methods exploiting both the small secret and 25-sparse error may be faster;
large error amplitudes affect those alternatives but not the clean probability.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` with a two-second cap and
recovered no witness. That censored outcome only rules out an immediate
recovery by this configuration; it does not estimate primal cost or compare it
with 2629 seconds. This remains an attack upper-bound model, not a proven
winner or lower bound. Dense uniform matrices do not invoke the
sparse-LWE boundary, and no Gaussian target-error map transfers a binary-
secret/entropy-LWE theorem to this sparse bounded distribution. Calibration is
`extrapolated`, measured runtime is null, and no hardness claim is made.
