## Interaction and alternative attacks

Enumeration covers every exact-weight secret accepted by the verifier, so its
candidate count is a matrix-independent complete upper bound.  Its practical
rate depends on dense column accumulation, `m=70`, rejection order, and the
513-value residual interval, which occupies roughly one quarter of the
modulus.  The recorded `1/n` rate scaling does not price those interactions.

A 15+16 split contains 22,819 and 39,203 partial supports of weights at most
eight.  Nonzero noise prevents a direct exact-key MITM join, while an
approximate four-row join has `513^4=69,257,922,561` possible error offsets.
MITM is unpriced and could still win with a better filtering strategy, but the
dossier has no measurement establishing that crossover.  Since one error
coordinate is zero with probability `1/513`, an all-clean 31-row subsystem has
probability `513^-31 = 2^-279.087`; clean-subset solving is not the selected
route.  Primal BDD and lattice-hybrid approaches are also unmeasured competing
experiments.  The loose full-secret fallback is
`2053^31 = 2^341.109` candidates; it is an upper bound, not a hardness floor or
a Lattice Estimator result.
