## Analytical model and placement

The literature route is the full-guess specialization of
[Son--Cheon 2019, Section 3.1, Algorithm 1](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/1019.pdf):
guessing the whole sparse secret removes the residual lattice stage and leaves
enumeration of the exact public support/value domain followed by witness
validation.  The checked-in `sparse_secret_enum` is an independent clean-room
implementation of only that degenerate component.  Because it traverses every
secret in the public predicate, the narrow mapping is independent of `A` and
the error law and is paper-case eligible here; it transfers no lattice
preprocessing, Gaussian success analysis, paper constant, or claim of fidelity
to the complete Algorithm 1.

The selected exact work factor is

`C(43,5) * 2^5 = 30,803,136 = 2^24.876574` candidates.

The `analytical-secret-enumeration-v1` formula is
`binomial(n,h)*nonzero_alphabet_size^h/(2.4e+06/n)`. It records
`nonzero_alphabet_size=2`, `measured_candidate_rate_n_product=1,200,000`, and
`rate_model=2*measured_candidate_rate_n_product/n`, hence an assumed rate of
`2,400,000/43 = 55,813.953488` candidates/second. The prediction is
`30,803,136/55,813.953488 = 551.889520000` seconds, in `E4=[256,1024)` and
close to the separate 548.748012819-second design target. The named rate
product is a cross-instance model parameter, not a measured runtime.

## Interaction and alternative attacks

Enumeration is a complete upper bound for the accepted secret language and its
candidate count is independent of `A`. Its practical rate is not: the dense
94-by-43 validation, rejection depth, column caching, and the 65-value residual window
all affect it, while the model's `1/n` scaling does not independently price
those effects or `m`.

A 21+22 split contains 758,451 and 973,017 signed partial assignments through
weight five. Exact-key joining is invalid with nonzero error, and even four
approximate coordinates admit `65^4=17,850,625` offsets. MITM is unpriced and
may beat enumeration with a better filter, but no crossover is measured and a
naive join pays material collision and memory costs. The Gaussian table's zero
mass is about 0.0374872, so an all-clean 43-row subsystem is only about
`2^-203.711`; clean-subset recovery is not primary. Primal BDD and lattice
hybrids remain unmeasured alternatives. The loose full-secret fallback is
`257^43 = 2^344.242` candidates, another upper bound rather than a hardness
floor or estimator result.

## Calibration and lower-bound scope

[`CALIBRATION.md`](../CALIBRATION.md) lists no selected-path recovery run for
this E4 release-byte record; E4--E5 were not run in that recovery campaign.
The separate two-second all-easy `primal_bdd` screen found no recovery, but it
is only a bounded smoke test. Accordingly the checked-in metadata remains
`calibration_status=extrapolated` with `measured_runtime_seconds=null`.

Exact secret min-entropy is 24.876574 bits and is directly exhausted by the
selected model. The named planted Gaussian does not by itself instantiate all
[Brakerski--Döttling entropy-LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119)
preconditions or a complete finite-parameter reduction. Dense uniform `A` is
outside the matrix-sparse scope of the cited
[sparse-LWE lower bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512). Neither scope check
rules out a faster attack, and `E4` is not a cryptographic hardness claim.
