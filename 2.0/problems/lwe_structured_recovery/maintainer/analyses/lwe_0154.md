## Interaction and limits of the claim

Uniform q-ary `A` in the estimator is a substitute for, not an analysis of, this sparse public matrix. The result is a heuristic proxy, not a reduction-backed upper bound and not a hardness claim. Signed cancellation, sparse column updates, and residual-window bucketing could all improve a mixed attack. This realization has no identical row support, so immediate same-support subtraction is unavailable; `C(100,20)=2^68.861` merely quantifies the raw support universe.

The selected `k=20=ceil(2*sqrt(100))` gives `k^2/n=400/100=4.0000`, so the BBTV finite retained-sample factor `1-3/l-k^2/n` is negative for every positive `l`. It is also not `o(sqrt(n))`, placing it outside the premise of the [sparse-LWE lower-bound result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512), and the planted balanced exact-weight signed secret is not the theorem's uniform q-ary target. BBTV is not instantiated; the structured secret is varied at the same time, interacting effects are explicitly unresolved, and no hardness claim is made. Generator entropy is 67.039 bits and verifier-language entropy is 69.294 bits; no [entropy-LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119) lower-bound transfer is claimed. Because the estimate is only 2.707 hours below the H4 ceiling, modest modeling error could move the practical runtime into H5; the bin is a calibrated target, not a guarantee.
