## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

Clean-subset recovery solves directly for an arbitrary q-ary secret and has no
secret structure to exploit. It uses error support but treats the 20-sparse
matrix only through a nominal family rate. Sparse row/error interaction,
sparse elimination, support-graph methods, and decoding the 200-dimensional
left-kernel syndrome can change the attack crossover even without repeated
supports.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` with a two-second cap and
recovered no witness. That censored non-recovery is only smoke evidence; it
does not estimate primal cost or rank it against 2983 seconds. The clean-
subset estimate is not a demonstrated portfolio minimum or a hardness lower
bound. Here `20/sqrt(100)=2`, not
`o(sqrt(n))`, so the cited sparse-LWE lower-bound premise is deliberately
absent. Calibration is `extrapolated`, measured runtime is null, and no
hardness claim is made.
