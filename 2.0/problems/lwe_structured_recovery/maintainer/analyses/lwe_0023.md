## Analytical model and placement

The literature route is the full-guess specialization of
[Son--Cheon 2019, Section 3.1, Algorithm 1](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/1019.pdf):
guessing the whole sparse secret removes the residual lattice stage and leaves
enumeration of the exact public support/value domain followed by witness
validation.  The checked-in `sparse_secret_enum` is an independent clean-room
implementation of only that degenerate component.  Because it traverses every
secret in the public predicate, the narrow mapping is independent of `A` and
the error law and is paper-case eligible here; it transfers no lattice
preprocessing, Gaussian success analysis, paper constant, or claim of fidelity
to the complete Algorithm 1.

The selected exact domain is

`C(83,3) * 2^3 = 735,048 = 2^19.487479` candidates.

The `analytical-secret-enumeration-v1` formula is
`binomial(n,h)*nonzero_alphabet_size^h/(2.4e+06/n)`. With
`nonzero_alphabet_size=2`, `measured_candidate_rate_n_product=1,200,000`, and
`rate_model=2*measured_candidate_rate_n_product/n`, it assumes
`2,400,000/83 = 28,915.662651` candidates/second. The resulting prediction is
`735,048/28,915.662651 = 25.420410000` seconds, in `E2=[16,64)` and close to
the separate 25.992076683-second design target. The named rate product belongs
to a cross-instance analytical model and is not this record's wall-clock time.

## Interaction and alternative attacks

Enumeration covers the entire accepted secret language, independently of the
matrix distribution. Its real cost still depends on dense column summation,
174-row validation, early rejection, and the 513-value residual interval; the
spec's `1/n` rate law does not independently model those constants or `m`.

A 41+42 split has 88,643 and 95,369 signed partial assignments through weight
three. Direct exact-key matching is invalid with nonzero error, while four
approximate coordinates admit `513^4=69,257,922,561` offsets. MITM is unpriced
and might win with a stronger filtering scheme, but a naive join has material
collision and memory costs. The Gaussian table's zero mass is about 0.00468752,
so an all-clean 83-row subsystem is only about `2^-642.168`; clean-subset
recovery is not the selected route. Primal BDD and lattice hybrids remain
unmeasured alternatives. Full-secret enumeration,
`2053^83 = 2^913.292`, is only a loose upper bound and not a hardness floor.

`CENSORED` at the 16-second lower-edge cap. The subsequent full E2 replay in

retained no portable per-instance wall time, so the public spec remains
`calibration_status=extrapolated` with `measured_runtime_seconds=null`.

Exact secret min-entropy is 19.487479 bits and is explicitly exposed by the
enumeration model. The named planted Gaussian alone does not instantiate every
[Brakerski--Döttling entropy-LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119)
precondition or a complete finite-parameter reduction map. Dense uniform `A`
is outside the matrix-sparse scope of the cited
[sparse-LWE lower bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512). These fail-closed
scope statements do not rule out other attacks, and `E2` is a modeled bin,
not a hardness claim.
