## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

Because a clean nonsingular square system recovers the whole secret over
`F_257`, this route never searches the binary domain. It intentionally ignores
124 bits of iid secret structure. Enumeration, small-secret hybrids, and joint
methods that combine binary coordinates with a 17-sparse error may outperform
the modeled route. Error magnitudes affect validation and decoding attacks but
not the clean-subset probability.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` with a two-second cap and
recovered no witness. This censored non-recovery is only smoke evidence; it
does not estimate primal cost or rank it against clean-subset. The E3 estimate
is therefore not a certified portfolio minimum or a hardness lower bound.
Dense-uniform `A` does not meet a sparse-matrix premise, while the
exact-support bounded error has no instantiated Gaussian mapping for a
binary-secret/entropy-LWE reduction. Calibration is `extrapolated`, measured
runtime is null, and no hardness assertion follows.
