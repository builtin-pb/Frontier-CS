## Interaction and limits of the claim

The estimator assumes uniform q-ary `A`, so its application to row-weight-21 `A` is a heuristic proxy. It is not a reduction-backed upper bound for this structured matrix and is not a hardness claim. The degree spread 10--32 is wider than in neighboring points and could make low-degree-coordinate guessing better on this particular seed. Still, there are no repeated supports for exact row differencing. The possible row supports number `C(110,21)=2^73.996`, which does not preclude partial-support algorithms.

The row weight `k=21=ceil(2*sqrt(110))` gives `k^2/n=441/110=4.0091`, making BBTV's finite retained-sample factor `1-3/l-k^2/n` negative for every positive `l`. This is also outside the `k=o(sqrt(n))` regime of the [sparse-LWE lower-bound result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512), while the planted exact-weight binary secret is not its uniform q-ary target. BBTV is not instantiated; the matrix-only result would not isolate this mixed distribution anyway, and no hardness claim is made. Secret entropy is 57.350 bits and is explicitly attacked; no [entropy-LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119) reduction is used. The H5 estimate depends chiefly on whether the uniform-A hybrid proxy survives the realized degree imbalance and on the `10^6` rop/second normalization.
