## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

An invertible clean subsystem recovers the arbitrary q-ary secret directly;
there is no secret enumeration structure to exploit. The model uses sparse
error support but otherwise ignores the sparse row graph. Sparse matrix/error
interaction, faster sparse elimination, and decoding a 98-dimensional
left-kernel syndrome remain live alternatives even though repeated supports
are absent in this seed.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` with a two-second cap and
recovered no witness. This censored non-recovery is only smoke evidence, not a
primal cost estimate or ranking against 157.908 seconds. The clean-subset
number is not certified as the portfolio minimum and is not a lower bound.
Here `14/sqrt(49)=2`, outside the `k=o(sqrt(n))` premise
of the cited sparse-LWE lower-bound regime. Calibration is `extrapolated`,
measured runtime is null, and no hardness claim is made.
