The concrete matrix has full rank `75/75`.  Its rows have `72--75` nonzeros

unbalanced-sign superset.  The smaller domain is valid only as a planted-secret

with a `5,761.995`-second target.  An all-clean dimension-size subset would
have probability `65^-75 = 2^-451.678`, leaving no competitive clean-system

The estimator is fed exact distribution models for this plant: uniform `A`,
`SparseTernary(7,7)`, and `Uniform(-32,32)`.  The uSVP work estimate itself and
the seconds normalization are heuristic, not a completed attack timing.  Nor
does the planted-law model prove or rule out a valid unbalanced alternate under
the broader verifier.

Min-entropy of the balanced plant is `60.739` bits.  The bounded-uniform error
does not supply the Gaussian target map required by the
[Brakerski--Dottling entropy-LWE theorem](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119), and
no finite-parameter reduction is claimed.  `H0` is therefore a calibration
label rather than hardness evidence.  The dense-uniform matrix also makes the
matrix-sparse lower-bound regime inapplicable.
