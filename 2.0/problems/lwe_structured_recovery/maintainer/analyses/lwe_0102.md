## Clean-subset model and bin

`p = C(137,50) / C(150,50) = 0.00388488089406335`.

This route is explicitly an adaptation of
[Sun--Tibouchi--Abe, ePrint 2020/666, Section 4.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/666):
choose an error-free subset, solve its linear system, validate the candidate,
and retry. With exactly 13 planted dirty rows, the event here is the exact
hypergeometric `C(m-w,n)/C(m,n)` displayed above. The paper's uniform-matrix
rank behavior and asymptotic runtime do not transfer to this dense binary

## Interaction, alternatives, and bounded screen

bitsets, yet Gaussian elimination remains over `F_769`. With half-dense rows,
the cited sparse-LWE lower-bound regime is irrelevant; with a full uniform
modular secret, no low-entropy-secret lower bound is needed or invoked.

does not import standard uniform-A hardness. No reduction claim is made.

`C(150,13)*192^13 = 2^159.271307`, compared with

alternate, although the combinatorial count is not a decoder runtime. Primal
BDD and binary-row combination attacks remain in scope.
