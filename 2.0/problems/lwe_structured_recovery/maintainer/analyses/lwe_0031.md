uniformly from `[-32,32]`, and acceptance caps are `L_inf=32`, `L_1=3,616`,
`L_2^2=115,712`, and residual support 113.

are 49 zeros among 11,865 positions, hence density `0.995870`.  In particular,
neither the least-supported row nor the least-supported column is a useful

`C(105,14) C(14,7) = 314,881,255,304,318,102,400 = 2^68.093` candidates.  The

times the trials.  This guards correctness for an unbalanced alternate even
