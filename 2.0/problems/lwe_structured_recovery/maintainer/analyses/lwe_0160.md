## Interaction and limits of the claim

The estimator's uniform q-ary matrix is not the released sparse matrix. The calculation is a heuristic normal-LWE proxy, neither a reduction-backed upper bound nor a hardness claim. At this small dimension, the 65/257 residual acceptance window, sparse support graph, and signed cancellations can shift practical crossover points substantially. This seed supplies no repeated support for direct row differencing; plain support exhaustion would span `C(75,18)=2^56.408`, but more selective methods remain possible.

Here `k=18=ceil(2*sqrt(75))`, so `k^2/n=324/75=4.3200` and BBTV's finite retained-sample factor `1-3/l-k^2/n` is negative for every positive `l`. The matrix is also outside the `o(sqrt(n))` premise of the [sparse-LWE lower-bound result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512), and the planted balanced exact-weight signed secret is not its uniform q-ary target. BBTV is not instantiated; the simultaneous signed secret makes a matrix-only conclusion inappropriate, and no hardness claim is made. Planted entropy is 60.739 bits and verifier-language entropy is 62.994 bits; both are explicit, and no [entropy-LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119) reduction is used. Because uSVP beats hybrid by only 0.184 bits in the model, the H0 label should be read as an extrapolated target whose ordering can change under sparse-matrix-aware implementations.
