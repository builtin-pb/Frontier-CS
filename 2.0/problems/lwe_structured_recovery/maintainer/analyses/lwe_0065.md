retry route in [Sun--Tibouchi--Abe, ePrint 2020/666,

clean-minor rank and family throughput remain modeled uncertainties.

`calibration_status="extrapolated"` and `measured_runtime_seconds=null`.

## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

The secret has no small-domain structure: clean equations solve for an
arbitrary vector over `F_1229`. The selected model therefore exploits error
sparsity alone. It does not credit interaction between the 11-sparse row graph
and the error support, nor syndrome decoding in the 54-dimensional left
kernel; either can alter runtime despite the lack of repeated supports.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` with a two-second cap and
recovered no witness. That capped outcome is only smoke evidence; it does not
estimate primal cost or compare it with 723.992 seconds. The clean-subset
number remains a modeled median estimate rather than a proven winner or lower
bound. Since `11/sqrt(27)=2.11695`, row weight is a constant
multiple of `sqrt(n)`, not `o(sqrt(n))`; the cited sparse-LWE lower-bound
premise is not met. Calibration is `extrapolated`, measured runtime is null,
and no hardness conclusion is made.
