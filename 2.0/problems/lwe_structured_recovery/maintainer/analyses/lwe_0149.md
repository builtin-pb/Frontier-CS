## Interaction and limits of the claim

This estimator result models uniform `A`; it does not analyze the released weight-25 rows. It is therefore a heuristic comparator, neither a reduction-derived upper bound nor a hardness assertion. Sparse multiplication limits a full pass to 3,950 active cells and may make hybrid guessing materially cheaper. The matrix's lack of repeated supports removes one elementary cancellation route, but the support graph and modular coefficient labels remain available for specialized algorithms. Exhausting row-support choices alone would span `C(150,25)=2^93.982`, which is descriptive rather than a lower bound.

The choice `k=25=ceil(2*sqrt(150))` gives `k^2/n=625/150=4.1667`. This makes BBTV's finite retained-sample factor `1-3/l-k^2/n` negative for every positive `l`, in addition to missing the `k=o(sqrt(n))` premise of the [sparse-LWE lower-bound result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512). The planted exact-weight binary secret is also not the theorem's uniform q-ary target. BBTV is therefore not instantiated, and no matrix-only conclusion or hardness claim is drawn. The secret has only 63.957 bits of exact support entropy and is directly enumerable; no [entropy-LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119) hardness transfer is claimed. H9 placement is dominated by the normalization of the uniform-A estimator, with sparse/secret interaction and early-rejection constants left as explicit research targets.
