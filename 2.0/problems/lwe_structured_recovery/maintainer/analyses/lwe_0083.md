## Clean-subset model and bin

There are 210 planted-clean rows. Therefore

`p = C(210,75) / C(225,75) = 0.00178442066989761`.

This route is explicitly an adaptation of
[Sun--Tibouchi--Abe, ePrint 2020/666, Section 4.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/666):
choose an error-free subset, solve its linear system, validate the candidate,
and retry. With exactly 15 planted dirty rows, the event here is the exact
hypergeometric `C(m-w,n)/C(m,n)` displayed above. The paper's uniform-matrix
rank behavior and asymptotic runtime do not transfer to this sparse signed
public matrix; selected-minor rank and solve throughput remain
instance/repository assumptions.

The exact reciprocal is `1/p=560.4060`; the catalog's work convention is
`ln(2)/p=388.443819` attempts. SA_SMALL uses the extrapolated family rate
`max(100*(16/75)^1.5,5)=9.853444594169613` systems/second. The quotient is
`39.422134641313235` seconds, in E2's `[16,64)` band and near the
`39.39662122703732`-second target. This is a 50%-success median under the
idealized independent-trial model, not a per-seed prediction or runtime upper
bound. The model also omits rank-deficient sampled minors and validation
overhead.
