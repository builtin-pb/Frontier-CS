## Literature route and final-byte calibration

This is a clean-room adaptation of the error-free-subset, linear-solve, and
retry route in [Sun--Tibouchi--Abe, ePrint 2020/666,
Section 4.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/666). For this fixed-weight release,
the exact event is `C(m-w,n)/C(m,n)`, here
`C(177,63)/C(189,63)`. The paper's uniform-matrix rank analysis and its
asymptotic runtime are not transferred to this finite instance; clean-minor
rank and the family-rate scaling remain local uncertainties.

The final-byte campaign in [`CALIBRATION.md`](../CALIBRATION.md) records a
successful `bounded_error` recovery with seed 9 in 3.874551 internal seconds;
the recovered vector passed the public verifier.  This is 0.125449 seconds
below E1's nominal four-second lower edge on the calibration host, so it is
retained as a small empirical edge miss alongside the 6.497-second analytical
median, not concealed or promoted to a portable bound. Because that bounded
campaign is not a complete E0--E5 benchmark, the public record still
states `calibration_status="extrapolated"` and
`measured_runtime_seconds=null`.

## Structure, alternatives, and boundary

Clean-subset recovery solves for an unrestricted field vector before applying
the binary predicate, so it does not search the `2^63` secret space. The model
therefore ignores the iid-binary structure. Exact binary enumeration,
binary-secret hybrid methods, and joint secret/error attacks remain possible;
the 256 error bound affects their filters even though clean-subset probability
depends only on the 12-position support.

The repository-level smoke screen ran `primal_bdd` with a two-second cap and
recovered no witness. This is only censored evidence against immediate
recovery in that configuration, not a primal cost estimate or comparison with
the 6.497-second model. That model remains a 50%-success median-runtime
estimate rather than a demonstrated minimum, lower bound, or upper bound.
Dense-uniform `A` does not invoke sparse-LWE theory,
and the exact-support bounded error is not the Gaussian target mapping needed
to transfer binary-secret or entropy-LWE results. Calibration is explicitly
`extrapolated`; the public measured-runtime field is null and no hardness
conclusion is drawn.
