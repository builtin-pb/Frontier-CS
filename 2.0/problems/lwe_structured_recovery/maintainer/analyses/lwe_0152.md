## Interaction and limits of the claim

This normal-LWE estimate replaces sparse `A` by uniform `A`. It is explicitly a heuristic proxy, not an upper-bound reduction or a hardness statement. Sparse rows make signed partial sums cheaper, and opposite signs permit cancellations that a binary-support count misses. The realized graph has no identical support to subtract directly, but specialized MITM and graph-based methods remain plausible. The row-support universe is `C(85,19)=2^61.884`, a descriptive count rather than a security bound.

The matrix is also outside the `o(sqrt(n))` hypothesis of the [sparse-LWE lower-bound result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12512), while the planted balanced exact-weight signed secret is not its uniform q-ary target. Both sides are structured here, so BBTV is not instantiated and no independent-effects or hardness claim is made. Planted entropy is 63.500 bits, whereas the verifier language has 65.755 bits; both are shown explicitly and no [entropy-LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119) reduction is invoked. Practical placement is most sensitive to sparse/signed interaction, residual-bucket collisions, and the estimator-to-seconds normalization.
